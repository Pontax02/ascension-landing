You can delete your Ascension account and your data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

## From the app

1. Open Ascension and sign in.
2. Go to the **Settings** tab.
3. In the **Account** section, tap **Delete account**.
4. Enter your **current password** and tap **Delete account**.
5. In the "Delete your account?" dialog, confirm by tapping **Delete account**.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

## Without the app, by email

If you have already uninstalled the app or can't sign in, email us **from the address you signed up with** at [ascensionapp.es@gmail.com](mailto:ascensionapp.es@gmail.com?subject=Account%20deletion%20request&body=Username%3A%20) with the subject "Account deletion request" and include your username.

We will delete your account and your data **within 30 days at most** and let you know by email once it's done. To protect your account, we only process requests sent from the email address linked to it.

## What gets deleted

- Your account: email, username and password (stored encrypted).
- Your progress: your habit tree, your completed-habit history, XP, level and streak.
- Your friendships and any friend requests you sent or received.

## What we keep

- If you made an in-app purchase, we keep the Google Play **purchase receipt** (order ID, product, date and purchase token) **unlinked from your account**. We need it so the same payment can't unlock another account and so we can process refunds.
- Welcome survey answers, which are anonymous and were never linked to your account (see the Privacy Policy).

If you have any questions, email us at [ascensionapp.es@gmail.com](mailto:ascensionapp.es@gmail.com).
