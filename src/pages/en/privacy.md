This Privacy Policy describes how Ascension Project ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Ascension mobile application ("the App").

## 1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect the following personal information:

- **Email address** — used for account authentication
- **Username** — used to identify your profile within the app

We also collect usage data generated by your activity in the app, such as habit completion records, XP progress, levels, and streaks. This data is used solely to provide the app's functionality.

## 2. Welcome Survey

The first time you open the App, we ask you to answer a short set of questions (for example, how you heard about Ascension). Your answers are collected anonymously and are not linked to your account or your identity in any way, and therefore do not constitute personal data.

We use these answers, in aggregate, for market research purposes and to improve how we reach new users. Because these answers are never linked to an identifiable individual, they are retained in aggregate form without an individual deletion request applying to them; the rights described in Section 6 do not apply to this anonymous data, since we have no way of identifying which answers belong to which person.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

- Create and manage your user account
- Provide the habit tracking and progression features of the app
- Calculate your XP, level, and streak statistics
- Improve the app's performance and user experience
- Understand how users discover the app and inform our market research, using the anonymous survey answers described in Section 2

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers hosted by Railway (railway.app). Passwords are encrypted using BCrypt before being stored. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is never used for advertising purposes.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below. This does not apply to the anonymous survey answers described in Section 2, which are not linked to your account.

## 7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Right of access** — you can request a copy of your personal data
- **Right to rectification** — you can request correction of inaccurate data
- **Right to erasure** — you can request deletion of your personal data
- **Right to data portability** — you can request your data in a portable format
- **Right to object** — you can object to the processing of your personal data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at [ascensionapp.es@gmail.com](mailto:ascensionapp.es@gmail.com).

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
